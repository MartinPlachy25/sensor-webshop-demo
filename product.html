            Made for the rounds you do every day.

            Point, pull the trigger, and the M20W shows where heat is building up: in a fuse box, a motor bearing or a window frame. The picture goes straight to your phone over Wi-Fi, so the report is ready before you leave the site.

            Every camera we sell is set up and tested in Mönchengladbach. We calibrate it every two years and repair it in our own workshop, so you are never without a camera for long.
