    Which camera is right for you?

    The question we hear most. Start with resolution: it decides how much detail you see, and how far away you can stand. Then the answers to what customers ask us every day.

    Try the resolution guide at the top of the camera range

    Which camera for checking my house?

    For a quick check at home, 256 × 192 is enough: the Pocket2, or the Mini X on your phone. For energy audits and reports for customers, choose 384 × 288 or more, for example the M31 or G31.

    Which camera for electrical and industrial inspection?

    The M series is made for daily rounds, from the M11W to the M60 with 640 × 480. The G series adds a laser range finder, GPS and interchangeable lenses. The SP series is the professional class with viewfinder and tilting screen, up to the SP120H with 1280 × 1024.

    What do H and W in the name mean?

    H stands for high temperature: the G41H and G61H measure up to 2000 °C, the SP40H, SP60H, SP100H and SP120H up to 2200 °C, for furnaces, glass and metal. W (M11W, M20W) means focus free with a wide lens: point and shoot. Models without W have manual focus and a narrower lens for more detail at a distance.

    Can a thermal camera see through glass or walls?

    No. Glass reflects heat like a mirror, so on a window you see a reflection, not what is behind it. On a wall you see the surface temperature, and that surface tells you what happens inside: a hidden pipe, missing insulation or damp. Try it above in the bathroom.

    What is thermal sensitivity (NETD)?

    The smallest temperature difference the camera can show. Lower is better. Below 40 mK shows differences of 0.04 °C, enough for most work. Below 20 mK, as on the SP100H and SP120H, shows the faintest contrasts, such as early damp.

    Wide or narrow lens?

    A wide lens sees a whole wall from close up, ideal inside rooms. A narrow lens shows more detail far away, such as roofs or overhead lines. The G and SP series take interchangeable lenses, so one camera does both.

    How often does a camera need calibration?

    Every two years, so it keeps measuring correctly. We calibrate in our own technical lab and remind you when it is due.

    Can my company pay on invoice?

    Can I rent a camera first?

    Yes, for one job or a whole season, calibrated and ready to use. Tell us what you want to inspect and we suggest the right model.
