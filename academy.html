Every camera comes with a person.

A thermal camera is only as good as the person reading the picture. That is why we do not just send you a box.

Whichever camera you buy from us, from the Mini X on your phone to the SP120, one of our experts shows you how to use it properly: the right settings, emissivity and distance, how to tell a real fault from a reflection, and how to turn a picture into a clear report.

And when you are standing in front of a switchboard or a damp wall and are not sure what you see, you call us. The same people who trained you pick up the phone.

  1. 01Personal introduction

    When your camera arrives, we walk you through it, online or here in Mönchengladbach.

  2. 02Practice on real cases

    Pictures from jobs we have done: heat loss, damp, loose contacts, worn bearings.

  3. 03Help after the course

    Questions from the field by phone, email or WhatsApp, from the people who know your camera.

Taught by the people who repair the cameras.

Our trainers are not presenters. They calibrate cameras in our own lab, repair them in our workshop and go out on jobs with customers. Measuring technology has been our family business since 1990.

So you learn what the manual leaves out: why a shiny pipe looks cold, when a warm spot is normal, and which setting saves you a second trip to the site.

    Good to know.

    Do I need to know anything before I start?

    No. The 3-hour and 1-day courses start from zero. For the 2- and 3-day courses it helps if you have used a thermal camera before, but it is not a must.

    Can I bring my own camera?

    Yes, please do. You learn fastest on the camera you will use every day. If you do not have one yet, add “Cameras for everyone” and we bring calibrated cameras for the group.

    Does it matter which camera I have?

    We teach on every HIKMICRO camera we sell, from the Mini X on your phone to the SP series. Tell us your model when you book, and the course is set up for it.

    In which languages do you teach?

    In German and English. Ask us about French.

    How does the online course work?

    You join a live video call in your browser, nothing to install. The trainer shows the camera and the software on screen, and you try along with your own camera.

    Can my company pay on invoice?

    Yes. You get a written offer first, and businesses pay on invoice after the course.

    Not sure which course fits?

    Tell us what you inspect and which camera you use. We suggest the right course and a date, without obligation.
